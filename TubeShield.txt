! Title: TubeShield — YouTube Ad Blocker for AdGuard

! --- WHAT THIS BLOCKS ---
! ✓ Feed/recommendation ads           ~85%

! ✓ Sidebar/companion ads              ~95%
! ✓ Ad tracking/telemetry domains      ~99%

! --- VIDEO AD LIMITATION ---
